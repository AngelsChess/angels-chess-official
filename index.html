Chess of the Angels

An original chess variant for the era of human–AI collaboration.

Can machines think? And if they can — can they be beaten?

The challenge

Chess is a finite, computable game — theoretically solvable, but not yet solved. What has been achieved is something more insidious: AI engines have accumulated so much knowledge that human intuition, creativity and experience no longer constitute a meaningful advantage. Chess of the Angels was designed in response. Its conditional activation mechanic introduces structural uncertainty that no engine can fully anticipate — because it depends entirely on the opponent's own decisions.

About the Work

Chess of the Angels preserves the objective and foundations of classical chess while introducing a 68-square board, four lateral Angel Wing squares and two new bicolour pieces per side — the Angels.

The Angels begin inactive. They acquire their movement and control when an opposing piece completes a legal move on the territorial frontier. Activation is collective, permanent and irreversible. The game returns initiative, intuition and creativity to the human player.

The Official Board

The board retains the 8×8 central grid and adds four playable lateral squares in ranks 4 and 5. File order from White's left: X, a, b, c, d, e, f, g, h, Y. The Angel is abbreviated G.

Registered European Union design: 68-square board — EUIPO No. 015110333-0001

The Angel Pieces

Each player begins with 18 pieces: 16 traditional plus 2 Angels. The White Angel has a light body and dark head; the Black Angel has a dark body and light head.

Why This Variant Matters

Expanded state space

The 68-square board, four additional pieces and the activation mechanic expand the state space by several orders of magnitude beyond classical chess.

Structural uncertainty

A position is not fully described by what is visible — it also depends on whether the Angels have been activated. No prior engine knowledge applies.

AI research value

Agents learning this variant from scratch must develop entirely new evaluation functions — a productive benchmark for reinforcement learning.

Human creativity restored

The initiative–response mechanic rewards anticipation and strategic patience in ways that pure calculation cannot replicate.
