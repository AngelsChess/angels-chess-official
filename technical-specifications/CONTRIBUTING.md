# Chess of the Angels

## Official Repository

**Chess of the Angels** is a chess variant designed for the era of Human–AI collaboration.

The project extends classical chess through:

- A 68-square board.
- Four Angel Wing squares.
- Two Angel pieces per player.
- Conditional territorial activation.
- A mandatory preliminary opening move.
- An expanded strategic state space.

Rather than replacing classical chess, Chess of the Angels explores new strategic, computational and philosophical dimensions while preserving the essential objective of checkmate.
